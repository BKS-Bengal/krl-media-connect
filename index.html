Adhyaksha

Smt. Rinku Majumder Ghosh was felicitated as Adhyaksha of the Mahila Wing, Bharatiya Krishak Samaj West Bengal. The badge sat on the pink sari for the rest of the morning.

Shri Debashish DharMLA, Sonarpur Uttar

Guest of Honour

Shri Debashish Dhar, MLA, Sonarpur Uttar, addressed the hall from the yellow-draped lectern.

What the stage carried

The argument of the morning, in each voice. Exact words from the recordings will replace these lines.

  1. The farmer is not a backdrop to policy. Krishi Ratna League puts the field at the centre of Bengal's next public contest.

    Shri Dudh Kumar Mondal

    Hon'ble Minister of Agriculture, West Bengal

  2. Half of Bengal's farm is a woman. The Mahila Wing is not a side room of the Samaj.

    Smt. Rinku Majumder Ghosh

    Adhyaksha, Mahila Wing, Bharatiya Krishak Samaj

  3. Protyabartan is not nostalgia. It is the village asking its children to stand with the soil again.

    Shri Debashish Dhar

    MLA, Sonarpur Uttar

  4. A league with a working farm at the end of it, not a trophy on a shelf.

    MahAcharya Shri Sourabh J. Sarkar

    State President, BKS West Bengal. KarmYog Ashram

Krishi Ratna League Bengal

Launch of Krishi Ratna League Bengal on the screen. MahAcharyaJi at the lectern. Reena, Rinku, Dhar seated.

The hall

The press huddle

After the programme the mics closed in. MahAcharyaJi, Smt. Rinku Majumder Ghosh, Shri Debashish Dhar and Smt. Reena J. Sarkar took the questions together. Bonglive, 24 Ghanta and the rest of the room stayed until the last answer.
